
# LIVELIHOODSAATHI AI
### Your Skills. Your Future. Your Voice.
**AI-Powered Multilingual Livelihood and Career Guidance**

### Problem Statement
Many students, job seekers, and underserved communities struggle to access personalized career and livelihood guidance. Language barriers, limited digital literacy, and fragmented skilling resources make it difficult to identify suitable career paths and training opportunities.

### Proposed Solution
LIVELIHOODSAATHI AI is a multilingual AI-powered livelihood advisory platform that helps users explore career pathways, identify skill-development opportunities, and access practical guidance through conversational interaction. Its voice-based and chat-based approach aims to make career support more accessible and user-friendly.

### Key Features
- Multilingual conversational assistance in Tamil, English, Hindi, and Tanglish.
- Voice and chat interaction for accessible communication.
- AI-powered career guidance and skill-development recommendations.
- Career roadmaps and information about relevant learning opportunities.
- Institutional tools for beneficiary and skilling-program management.

### Technology Stack
Next.js, React, TypeScript, Node.js, WebSocket, Google Gemini, machine-learning recommendation methods, and SQLite.

### Expected Impact
LIVELIHOODSAATHI AI aims to reduce language and digital-access barriers, help users make informed career decisions, and connect learners with relevant skill-development opportunities. It also aims to support organizations in managing livelihood and skilling initiatives more efficiently.
